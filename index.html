May 20, 2026
Arrival in Munich
We finally landed in Munich this morning! After settling into the accommodation, we headed straight to the Viktualienmarkt for some traditional Bavarian food. The weather is absolutely perfect. The Glockenspiel performance at noon was a great introduction to the city's charm. Tomorrow we plan to rent bikes and explore the English Garden.
May 23, 2026
Train to Würzburg
Saying goodbye to Munich today. We took the afternoon train to Würzburg. The scenery along the way was beautiful. Würzburg feels so different, surrounded by vineyards. The Alte Mainbrücke is incredible at sunset—everyone gathers there with a glass of local Franconian wine. Can't wait to see the Residenz tomorrow.
